# Song Reuploading
Here's how you reupload a song to a Private Server

1. Go to [Dropbox](https://www.dropbox.com/) and log in (or register if you haven't made your account yet)
2. Upload a song (.mp3)
3. After the song has been uploaded to your dropbox account, copy the link by tapping the dots

![](https://file.garden/ZSHQnWTvf253N27v/gdphps/re4.png) ![](https://file.garden/ZSHQnWTvf253N27v/gdphps/re.png) ![](https://file.garden/ZSHQnWTvf253N27v/gdphps/re3.png)

4. Go to this [link](https://gdph.ps.fhgdps.com/tools/songAdd.php), or alternatively, you can find it on the [tools](https://gdph.ps.fhgdps.com/tools/) website named songAdd.php

![](https://file.garden/ZSHQnWTvf253N27v/gdphps/re5alt.jpg)

5. Paste the song link inside the box
6. Then click "**Add song**"

![](https://file.garden/ZSHQnWTvf253N27v/gdphps/re6.jpg)
