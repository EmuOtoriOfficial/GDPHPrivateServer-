# App Updates on Android
Here's how you update the app every time The Host or Zyann announces a new update
1. Go to https://gdph.ps.fhgdps.com/
1. Tap "Download"
1. Download "GD Philippines (version).apk"
1. After the apk downloads, install it
1. Enjoy
