# Browsing Custom SFX
1. Go to SFX library as usual
1. Then go to "User uploads" to find Custom SFX
# Uploading Custom SFX to FHGDPS
1. Join GDPSFH Discord Server first before you'll be able to upload your Sound Effects - https://discord.gg/TyyqQGuWb8
1. Read the `#sfx-rules` text channel first before uploading your sound effect
1. After reading the rules, Go to `#bot-commands` text channel and type "/sfx add" then fill up what's required (SFX Name, and SFX File. Category is optional)
1. After You uploaded your SFX, you have to wait for mods to approve or deny your SFX submission
1. After mods accepted your SFX, You can now use it on GD
